StormCall — prototype review

A storm crew callout & onboarding system. This is a working prototype, not the finished product.

StormCall handles the scramble after a vendor storm call comes in: figuring out who's available, texting them all at once, tracking who says yes, and getting the ones who accept fully onboarded before they roll out.

Open the prototype →
Opens in this window. Works on desktop and phone.
Nothing here is real. The roster is 90 fabricated workers with fake (555) numbers, all data lives in the browser and resets when you reload, and no text messages are actually sent — the SMS flow is simulated so you can walk through it end to end. Rates, vendor names and addresses are placeholders.

What to look at

The six tabs across the top are the whole system. A sensible path through it:

  1. Roster — the pool of workers, filterable by classification, region and availability.
  2. New Storm Call — set how many of each classification you need, fill in the event details, and send the blast.
  3. Worker Phone (demo) — see what a lineworker actually receives, and reply YES or NO as them.
  4. Live Event — the fill board. Watch classifications fill up as replies come in. There's a button to simulate a wave of responses.
  5. Onboarding Portal (demo) — what a worker gets after accepting: direct deposit, W-9, PPE sizes, cert uploads.
  6. Denial Log — everyone who declined, timestamped.

What would be most useful to hear back

  • Are the seven classifications right, or is something missing?
  • Does the storm call form capture everything a real callout needs?
  • Is the text a worker receives clear enough to act on from a phone, in bad conditions?
  • Anything in the onboarding step that would trip up a worker or create a problem on our end?

Where this is in the process

The direction is approved and the prototype is built. Next is the production version — real database, real texting through Twilio, real roster.

The gating item isn't the software. Carriers require a registration (A2P 10DLC) before any business can send this kind of text, and approval takes anywhere from a few days to three weeks. Worker consent has to be collected and documented before the first real blast too. Both are already underway in parallel with the build.
