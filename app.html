How the real flow works
1. You hit Send — Twilio blasts the storm call to every matching, opted-in worker.
2. Worker replies YES → instant auto-reply with rates, per diem, staging details, and a one-time secure link.
3. Worker replies NO → logged to the Denial Log with a timestamp.
4. If their classification is already filled when the YES arrives, they get a “roster full — you're on standby” message instead.
5. The link opens their onboarding page (next tab) where they submit payment info, W-9, sizes, and certs.
6. STOP/HELP keywords and opt-out handling are automatic through Twilio.
